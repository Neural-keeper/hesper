## HYG v4.4 star catalog

- **Data:** [HYG v4.4](https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg/src/branch/main/data/hyg/CURRENT/hyg_v44.csv.gz)
- **Repository revision at download:** `53e3df311869e813ace5f1ad2ec4ce909f13256c`
- **Downloaded to:** `hyg/hyg_v44.csv.gz`
- **Contents:** HYG combines Hipparcos, Yale Bright Star, and Gliese data
  and includes HIP identifiers plus RA/Dec positions. The compressed catalog
  contains 119,614 stars according to the upstream README.

  the attribution and share adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **License evidence:** [HYG LICENSE](https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg/src/branch/main/LICENSE)
  and [HYG README](https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg/src/branch/main/data/hyg/README.md)
